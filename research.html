Research areas

Where we focus.

Four programmes, each approached with the same standard: state the hypothesis, show the method, publish the code.

01 — Cross-sectional

Factor Models

Systematic approaches to understanding cross-sectional returns: how factors are defined, how robust they are across universes and periods, and how sensitive conclusions are to construction choices.

02 — Microstructure

Market Microstructure

Research into liquidity, execution, price formation, and market behaviour — the mechanics that determine whether an idea survives contact with real markets.

03 — Allocation

Portfolio Construction

Research into robust portfolio optimisation and risk management, with attention to estimation error, constraints, and the gap between in-sample and out-of-sample behaviour.

04 — Statistical learning

Machine Learning

Applications of statistical learning to quantitative research, with an emphasis on validation design, leakage, overfitting, and honest reporting of what does not work.

Publication standard

What every publication aims to include.

Hypothesis

The question, stated before the result, with what would count as evidence against it.

Method

Every modelling decision and assumption, documented in enough detail to be reproduced.

Data provenance

Where inputs come from, how they were processed, and known limitations or biases.

Code & limitations

The code that produced the result, and an honest section on what the work does not show.
