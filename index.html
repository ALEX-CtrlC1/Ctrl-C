Principles

Open by default.
Rigorous by design.

Openness is only valuable when the work is worth inspecting. We hold ourselves to both standards at once.

  1. 01 ⟨ ⟩

    Open Research

    Research should be inspectable, reproducible, and useful to the broader quantitative community.

  2. 02 ≡

    Reproducible Systems

    Build research and infrastructure that others can understand, test, and extend.

  3. 03 σ

    Quantitative Rigor

    Prioritise evidence, statistical discipline, robust methodology, and careful experimentation.

  4. 04 ∫ dt

    Long-Term Thinking

    Build durable technology and research rather than chasing short-term market narratives.

Infrastructure

Research becomes infrastructure.

A model is only one part of serious quantitative work. It also needs reliable data, infrastructure for experiments, realistic simulation, risk controls, monitoring, and systems that others can reproduce.

  1. 01

    Research

    Form a falsifiable hypothesis and state what would disprove it.

  2. 02

    Models

    Formalise the idea with explicit, documented assumptions.

  3. 03

    Data

    Versioned, point-in-time inputs with known provenance.

  4. 04

    Simulation

    Test against realistic costs, constraints, and regimes.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Implement carefully, with risk controls and monitoring.

  6. 06

    Evaluation

    Compare outcomes with expectations and record the difference.

Evaluation feeds back into research. The loop is the system.

About

We believe quantitative finance benefits from the same principles that transformed software: open collaboration, reproducibility, rigorous engineering, and shared infrastructure.

Much of quantitative finance is built behind closed doors. Methods go unexamined, results can't be reproduced, and the same infrastructure is rebuilt again and again in isolation.

CTRL+C takes a different approach. We publish our research and the systems behind it, so the work can be inspected, reproduced, challenged, and extended. We think openness makes research better, because work that others can check has to be careful.
